## Purpose

A 30-second screen-capture walkthrough of the Phase 1 capture flow, cut for silent autoplay on LinkedIn and as the hero asset in beta outreach.

**This is a second, separate asset** from the demo video already embedded on the landing page (`youtube.com/embed/Z4C_fkGVAmw`). That one lives on-site and can run longer. This one is built to stop a thumb in a feed.

## Audience

Must read equally to all four verticals named on the live site:

| Vertical | Their trigger |
|---|---|
| Real Estate | FSBOs, expired listings, distressed properties |
| Construction & Trades | Job sites, competitor trucks, permit signs, material deliveries |
| Landscaping & Exterior | Neglected lawns, new builds, maintained commercial properties |
| Field Sales | Businesses, signage, foot traffic across a territory |

**The common thread is the behavior, not the target:** you drive a territory, you spot something, you can't stop, you forget by dinner. Sell the behavior. Anything that names a specific trigger locks out three-quarters of the audience.

## Constraints

- **Runtime:** 30 seconds hard. Feed autoplay dies around 30–35s.
- **Sound-off first.** Assume the majority never hear the VO. Every beat must land on screen text alone.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 primary (LinkedIn, Stories). 1:1 secondary crop. Do not letterbox 16:9.
- **Capture source:** iPhone screen recording of the PWA — web-first beta, no native build (Decision 011).
- **Real data only.** No lorem ipsum, no placeholder addresses. Use a real street a viewer could plausibly drive.

## Voiceover

Delivery is flat and competent — a peer, not an announcer. No upsell inflection.

> You see it. You always see it.
>
> Then you keep driving — and by dinner, it's gone.
>
> *(beat — shutter)*
>
> Photo. Location's already there.
>
> "Third and Maple. Worth a call Thursday."
>
> Saved. And in three days, it taps you on the shoulder.
>
> Bobert. See what others drive past.

**Word count:** 48. Deliberately sparse — the screen action carries the middle, and the pauses are load-bearing. Do not pad to fill 30 seconds.

**On the dictated line:** *"Third and Maple. Worth a call Thursday"* is the neutral core of the script. It states a location and an intent and nothing else. A real estate agent, a roofer, a lawn crew, and a territory rep would each say something close to it. Any edit that adds a *reason* — a roof, a lawn, a listing — breaks neutrality. If this line changes, re-check it against all four verticals.

## Music

Low-tension bed, no drop, no build. This is a utility product — a track that swells will make it feel like a crypto ad. Cut music entirely at 0:27 so the wordmark lands in silence.

---

## Do Not

- **Do not name a vertical**, on screen or in VO. The moment the script says "listing" or "job site," the other three audiences stop watching.
- Do not show the map view. Out of Phase 1 scope; implies a feature the beta doesn't ship.
- Do not show more than three taps. The site promises "Three taps." The video must not contradict it.
- Do not recreate the wordmark in the editor. Use the approved PNG (DECISION 011).
- Do not recolor the mark or apply filters to it (DECISION 010, DECISION 011).
- Do not stage anything aspirational. No luxury property, no new equipment. The audience works ordinary territory and will read polish as fake.
