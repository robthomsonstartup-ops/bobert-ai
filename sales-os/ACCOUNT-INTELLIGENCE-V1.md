# Bobert Account Intelligence V1

An account is a living intelligence object, not a CRM record.

## Account Object

- Identity: canonical name, domains, locations, entity confidence
- People: contacts, roles, relationship history, identity confidence
- Business: scale, growth, locations, markets, ownership/development context
- Construction: projects, rollouts, renovations, developers, architects, GCs, ECs
- Buying path: specification, procurement, purchasing, approval, vendor requirements
- Signals: dated events that may affect timing or relevance
- Evidence: source-backed claims and original artifacts
- Unknowns: questions that materially affect strategy
- Activity: calls, emails, replies, research, meetings, outcomes
- Strategy: why the account matters and where to enter
- Next action: one explicit recommended move

## Evidence Priority

1. Original user-provided artifact
2. Primary company source
3. Government / permit / filing source
4. Reputable industry source
5. Secondary source
6. Model inference

Lower-priority evidence does not overwrite stronger contradictory evidence without review.

## Contact Intelligence

A contact is not considered the right person merely because their title sounds relevant. Bobert should establish, when possible:

- actual responsibility
- influence vs ownership
- relationship to construction/design/procurement
- likely buying or specification path
- evidence of relevance
- open questions

## Signal Handling

Signals are dated observations, not conclusions. A signal can increase priority without proving an opportunity.

Example:

SIGNAL: company announced 20 new stores.

INFERENCE: construction activity may increase.

UNKNOWN: whether this contact controls lighting.

NEXT RESEARCH: determine construction procurement/specification structure.

## Communication Gate

Before generating external communication, Bobert should identify which facts are safe to reference. Claims with UNKNOWN, INFERRED, or CONFLICTING status require either omission or explicit internal review.

## Learning Loop

A response such as "lighting is purchased through our electrical contractors" is both an activity outcome and market intelligence. Bobert should preserve the response, attach it to the account/contact, and allow the pattern to influence future strategy without pretending that one response proves an industry-wide rule.
