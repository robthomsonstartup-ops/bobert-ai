The problem

Your program’s story is scattered.

Stats in one app, the schedule somewhere else, video in a third place, and the history in somebody’s binder. None of it belongs to the program, and players disappear from view when they move on.

No single home

Families, recruits and alumni hunt across five places to find one answer.

History gets lost

Records, past seasons and great players fade when a coach or volunteer moves on.

No time to keep it up

Coaches coach. A site that depends on a weekly volunteer goes stale by midseason.

Families first

Built with player privacy in mind.

These sites are about real kids. We work inside the sign-off your families already give you, and we’ll give you the consent wording to use.

  • Basic roster info, covered by your registration sign-off. Name, number, position, team, grad year and stats.
  • Anything more, a parent opts in. Headshots, bios, highlights and commitments only with permission.
  • Never published: home address, phone, personal email, birthdate, grades or health information.
  • Removal on request. A parent can have their player’s information taken down, quickly.
  • Your records, your program. You own your data and your domain.
