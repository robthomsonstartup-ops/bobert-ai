The problem

Your program’s story is scattered.

Stats live on one site, the schedule on another, video somewhere else, and the history in a coach’s binder. None of it belongs to the program, and players disappear from view the day they graduate.

No single home

Families, recruits and alumni hunt across five places to find one answer.

History gets lost

Records, past seasons and great players fade when a coach or volunteer moves on.

No time to maintain it

Coaches coach. A site that depends on a weekly volunteer goes stale by May.

Families first

Built with student privacy in mind.

These sites are about real kids. We treat their information that way.

  • Directory-level information by default. Name, number, position, class year and stats.
  • Enhanced profiles need a parent’s signed consent. Headshots, bios and highlights only with permission.
  • Never published: home address, phone, personal email, birthdate, grades or health information.
  • Removal on request. A parent can have their player’s information taken down, quickly.
  • Built from your records. Rosters and stats come from the program, with its permission.
