The Core

From drawings to quote.

The first Bobert system is focused on fixing the quoting workflow from end to end. The objective is not to replace the person who knows lighting. It is to remove the repetitive work around the decisions.

01 / INTAKE

Bring in the project

RFQs, drawings, specifications, schedules and project information become the starting point.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Find the truth

Organize what the documents actually call for and turn fixture information into structured data.

03 / CONFIGURE

Apply your way

Use preferred manufacturers, alternates, product rules and company specific quoting logic.

04 / VERIFY

Keep humans in control

Surface uncertainty and exceptions so the people who know lighting make the final call.

05 / QUOTE

Get the package out

Move verified fixture configurations into a clean, structured package for the quoting workflow.

06 / REMEMBER

Make the next one easier

Capture verified decisions so every project can contribute to the company's private lighting memory.

The Bigger Idea

A lighting information OS.

Bobert starts with the quoting workflow because that is where the work, decisions and product knowledge come together. From there, the system can grow into a connected information layer for the organization.

Market→Opportunity→Project→Drawings→Configuration→Quote→Field→Memory

One system where the work creates the knowledge, and the knowledge helps the next project. Each organization can build its own private memory around the products, people, projects and decisions that make its business different.
