Your Program. Your Players. Your Story.

Example build · Program Home

Center Grove Baseball.

Our first Program Home build is a working prototype for the Center Grove High School baseball program in Greenwood, Indiana, on its own domain. It shows what any program gets: its whole story in one place that the program owns.

  • 21 seasons of history, back to the 2004–05 season
  • Permanent player profiles that stay with each player after graduation
  • Alumni by graduating class
  • Rosters, schedules, results and stats loaded from the program’s records
  • Verified news tagged to the players in it, plus photos
  • Built as a template: the next program is new data, not new code

How we work

One studio. Built around each customer.

Built for one customer at a time

Every product starts with one kind of person and one job they’re tired of doing. It gets its own name and look so it feels made for them, because it was.

AI does the heavy lifting

Reading documents, pulling records together and keeping things current. The tedious part is ours. The decisions stay with the customer.

Real information only

If we can’t back it up, we don’t show it. Every product says plainly what it knows and what it doesn’t.
