The core

From drawings to quote.

The first Bobert system is focused on fixing the quoting workflow from end to end. The goal is not to replace the person who knows lighting. It is to remove the repetitive work around the decisions.

01 / INTAKE

Bring in the project

RFQs, drawings, specifications, schedules and project information become the starting point.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Find the truth

Organize what the documents actually call for and turn fixture information into structured data.

03 / CONFIGURE

Apply your way

Use preferred manufacturers, alternates, product rules and company specific quoting logic.

04 / VERIFY

Keep humans in control

Surface uncertainty and exceptions so the people who know lighting make the final call.

05 / QUOTE

Get the package out

Move verified fixture configurations into a clean, structured package for the quoting workflow.

06 / REMEMBER

Make the next one easier

Capture verified decisions so every project can contribute to the company's private lighting memory.

The bigger idea

One system. Growing with the work.

Bobert is not being built as a collection of disconnected AI tools. The idea is to connect the information that moves through a lighting organization and let the work create the memory.

Market→Opportunity→Project→Drawings→Configuration→Quote→Field→Memory

Start with the quoting problem. Build outward from there. Each piece adds context to the next, and the information created along the way becomes part of the company's private knowledge.
